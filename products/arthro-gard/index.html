What it is

Arthro Gard Advanced Formula provides triple action to increase flexibility and mobility, support healthy joint cartilage production and maintain muscle strength to reduce strain on joint. Glucosamine, chondroitin and collagen type 2 make up the essential building blocks in the formation of joint cartilage — where healthy ones promote flexibility. Hyaluronic acid and chondroitin aids smooth movement and relieve stiffness. Methylsulfonylmethane (MSM), boswellia serrata 80% and CurQlife turmeric extract are potent anti-inflammatory agents which help to reduce pain, swelling and protects cartilage in joints which reduces joint stiffness and discomfort, thus improving quality of life. Arthro Gard Advanced Formula contains no artificial preservatives, no artificial colours, no artificial flavours, and is free from sodium, yeast, sugar, starch, gluten & GMO
