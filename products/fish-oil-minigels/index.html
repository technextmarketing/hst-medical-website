What it is

Adequate intake of omega-3 essential fatty acids provide numerous health benefits for the heart, brain, eyes and skin. EPA is essential for maintaining a healthy heart, healthy inflammation management, blood circulation and cholesterol levels while DHA plays an integral role in supporting healthy brain and eye function. As a whole, essential fatty acids reduce inflammation and joint swelling. Small & easy to swallow, each 300mg of fish oil mini soft gels delivers purely 180mg of EPA and 120mg of DHA, without any fillers. Fish Oil Minigels provides concentrated high quality and high strength omega-3 EPA and DHA using patented Omegatec®, a line of premium quality marine triglycerides sustainably sourced from GMO-free wild deep sea fishes such as mackerel and tuna, that are especially rich in omega-3 fatty acids. The process to extract, purify and concentrate the fish oil is environmentally friendly and creates zero waste. They contain no artificial preservatives, artificial colours, artificial flavours, starch, yeast, gluten or wax.
