What it is

The Rheuma-Salve® Pain Relief Patch (Cool) is a plaster that delivers fast-acting, cool-sensation relief directly to the source of pain. Each pack holds 8 soft, flexible 10 × 7 cm patches that are gentle on the skin and made for muscle aches, sprains, strains, joint pain, backaches, sports recovery and delayed onset muscle soreness.

How to use
  • How To Use: Apply to clean, dry skin 2 to 3 times daily; remove after 6 hours.
  • Caution: External use only; do not apply to open wounds or use during pregnancy/breastfeeding.
  • Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
Active ingredients
  • This proprietary formula leverages a synergistic blend of botanical extracts and essential oils to provide deep, cooling relief:
  • Peppermint Oil (6%): A natural analgesic for an immediate cooling and soothing sensation.
  • Menthol (5.8%): Delivers a targeted “fast relief” cooling effect, helping to dull pain signals in the affected area.
  • Centella Asiatica Leaf Extract (3%): Manages localised inflammation and supports circulation to ease muscle strain.
  • Hops Oil (1.5%): Enhances deep muscle comfort and combat inflammation.
  • Frankincense Oil (0.5%): Reduces joint swelling, ease muscle tension, and improve blood flow to accelerate recovery.
  • Myrrh Oil (0.5%): Blocks inflammation- causing chemicals and easing muscle stiffness.
  • Other Ingredients: Titanium Dioxide, Petrolatums, Resin. Contains Zinc Oxide for skin protection.
