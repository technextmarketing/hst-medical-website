What it is

HST Medical® Magnesium Glycinate is a combination of elemental magnesium with the amino acid glycine. This form of magnesium is highly bioavailable, enabling more efficient absorption through the small intestines. HST Medical® Magnesium Glycinate supports heart, nerve, muscle and bone health, promoting physical relaxation and improves sleep quality.
