What it is

In 1906, Dr. Tsujimoto discovered the presence of highly unsaturated hydrocarbon in the liver of deep-sea sharks. Since then, extracted squalene has been introduced as a natural health supplement. Heritage Deep Sea Squalene is made of 99.9% unsaturated oil extracts from the shark’s liver. Squalene is a rich source of antioxidants, which exerts protective and healing functions and it also facilitates the supply of oxygen in the body systems. It strengthens the immune system, promotes healthy skin and enhances overall well-being.

How to use
  • Oral Intake: Take 2 softgels daily with warm water. Topical Use: The contents of 1 to 2 softgels may be applied onto the skin after a warm shower, to relieve dry and itchy conditions.
