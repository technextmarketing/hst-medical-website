What it is

Flu Gard is 100% herbal and is based on a safe traditional Chinese medicine formula indicated for common cold & flu symptoms. It reduces fever, eases body aches, runny nose & sneezing, relieves cough, reduces phlegm , and detoxifies the respiratory system.
