What it is

AlgaOmega is derived from sustainably sourced Schizochytrium sp., a marine microalgae which produces plant-based EPA and DHA. Through an environmentally responsible process of cultivation and purification, it bears a consistently pure, naturally potent and effective ingredient for human nutrition. Using patented life’s®OMEGA 60 algae with Vitamin D and E, it supports optimal cognitive function and eye health, along with cardiovascular functionality and joint mobility.

How to use
