What it is

Heritage Lingzhi Cracked-Spores Plus is a daily herbal supplement designed to enhance overall well-being by providing increased energy and vitality. The product combines two powerful medicinal mushrooms: organic lingzhi cracked-spores and wild yunzhi concentrated extract. Organic lingzhi spores offer various health benefits, including strengthening the immune system, protecting the lungs, liver, and kidneys, as well as relieving chronic fatigue, chesty cough, shortness of breath, and lack of energy. The wild yunzhi extract contains active components such as polysaccharides peptides (PSP), known for supporting the immune system and providing relief from cough and wheezing.
