What it is

DocosaHexaenoic Acid or DHA is the most abundant omega-3 fatty acid in the brain and retina of the eye, and remains vital for brain and eye development and function, and heart health throughout life. DHA 600 is suitable for babies, children and adults. In babies and children, it supports brain, spinal cord, nerves and vision developments, healthy learning, memory and behavior and overall growth and development. In adults, DHA 600 promotes memory and cognitive health, supports eye health and reduces risk for age- related vision problems, supports heart health, and reduces risk of cardiovascular disease, and lowers triglyceride levels if combined with appropriate diet and lifestyle. It contains molecularly distilled fish oil with dioxins, PCBs and heavy metals removed, based on stringent pharmaceutical standards. It has no artificial preservatives, no artificial colours, no artificial flavours, is free from yeast, sugar, starch, gluten, wax, and cholesterol.

How to use
  • Above 12 years old: Take 1 to 2 softgels daily or as professionally prescribed. Below 12 years old: Take 1 softgel daily. Pregnant & breast-feeding mothers: Take 2 to 4 softgels daily. For babies & toddlers: Squeeze out the content of 1 soft gel and consume with some milk, water, juice or food.
