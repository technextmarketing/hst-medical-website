What it is

Ivy Leaf Cough Syrup is a natural formula to reduce coughing, soothe airways and clear mucus and phlegm for effective congestion and cough relief. It is non-drowsy, sugar-free, alcohol-free, has no added colouring, and comes in a pleasant natural blackcurrant flavour. Fortified with 3.5 billion lactobacilli per sachet for additional immunity support (probiotics are Lactobacillus Paracasei MCC1849 from Japan). Convenient and travel- friendly individual sachets for on-the-go cough relief.

How to use
