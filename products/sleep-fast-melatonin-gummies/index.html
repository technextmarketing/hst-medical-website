What it is

Sleep Fast Melatonin Gummies contain pharmaceutical grade melatonin which is a fast-acting drug free sleep aid to promote natural and quality sleep, resulting in better overall health. Melatonin helps regulate body’s internal clock and control your sleep and wake cycles. It inhibits melanin formation and increases sleep time for people with altered sleeping routines due to busy schedules. Sleep Fast Melatonin Gummies relax your mind, leaving you feel regenerated and refreshed upon waking up. Contains no artificial preservatives, colours, starch, or gluten.
