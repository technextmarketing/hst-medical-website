What it is

Clear Eye Plus is specially formulated with a combination of premium Chinese herbs and phytonutrients vitamin A, zinc, lutein, zeaxanthin and bilberry, to help maintain healthy eyes & vision, relieve eye fatigue, and support night vision. Recommended for individuals who experience eye strain, dryness, redness and blurred vision from prolonged digital screen time on laptop, computers, and smart phones.

How to use
