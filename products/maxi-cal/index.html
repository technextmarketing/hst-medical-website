What it is

Maxi-Cal is coral calcium in a vegetarian softgel and in liquid form to allow easy swallowing and faster absorption. It has been formulated with essential vitamin D to further enhance the uptake of calcium, and is free artificial preservatives, artificial colours, artificial flavours, and free from sodium, yeast, sugar, starch, gluten, & GMO. An essential element that maintains strong and healthy teeth and bones, muscles and nervous system, calcium is particularly important for growing children, pregnant and lactating women as well as for people with active lifestyles.

How to use
  • Take 2 vegi softgels daily with meals or as professionally prescribed. Suitable for Vegetarians • FSSC 22000 certified
