What it is

Zoo-Vite Multivitamin Gummies provide kids with a healthy head start for growth and development. These great tasting gummies made with 100% natural fruit flavour contain 13 essential vitamins and minerals and comes in fun gummy bear shapes. Zoo-Vite Multivitamin Gummies are free from artificial flavours, artificial colours, artificial preservatives, artificial sweeteners, animal gelatin, wheat (gluten), diary, peanuts, tree nuts and has sugar-free coating.
