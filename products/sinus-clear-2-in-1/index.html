What it is

An innovative 2 in 1 design combines an applicator and inhaler, serving two different needs in a small pocket-friendly lipstick-sized container, topped off with a key chain. Comprising of a detachable top and bottom component, the top serves as a refillable inhaler to provide relief from blocked and congested noses. The bottom component acts as both an temple applicator for headaches as well as a refill for the inhaler. Just a few drops from the applicator to the inhaler’s cotton stick allows it to be used again. Useful for those who have a cold, headache or both at the same time.

How to use
  • Breathe deeply using the inhaler for blocked nose. For headaches or mosquito bites, apply a few drops of oil from the applicator on affected area and massage gently until the medicated oil is absorbed into the skin. Single person use is recommended.
