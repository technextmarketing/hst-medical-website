What it is

Traditionally treasured for its tonic effects on various organ systems, cordyceps sinensis is one of the most sought after medicinal fungi in Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM). It promotes recovery, restores energy after illness, and when taken regularly, relieves fatigue and enhances general well-being. It is highly nutritional and is cultivated under controlled parameters to yield a strain that closely resembles the wild Tibetan cordyceps sinensis used in TCM.

How to use
  • Adults: Take 1 to 2 vegicaps, once or twice a day with warm water before meals. Children: (Under 12 years) Take 1 vegicap, once or twice a day with warm water before meals Alternative Use: Cordyceps CS-4 powdered contents may be added to soups or congee.
