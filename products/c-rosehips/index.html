What it is

Vitamin C is an essential nutrient and plays multiple roles to support and maintain overall health and total well-being. Vitamin C works as a potent water-soluble antioxidant and co-factor for many enzymes in the body. The human body is not able to make or store vitamin C, making it important to consume sufficient amounts through food and dietary supplements. HST Medical Time Release C provides 1000mg of vitamin C with rosehips. The time release system delivers small amounts of vitamin C over an extended time for better absorption by the body. Plant polyphenols in rosehips further enhance the benefits of vitamin C.
