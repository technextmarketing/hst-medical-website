Description

Vitamin D3 uses Vitamin K2 (Menaquinone-7), a bioactive form of Vitamin K2 which can be easily absorbed by our body. It is essential for promoting the absorption of calcium and phosphorus from our digestive tract. Both elements are vital in the formation, growth and maintenance of strong and healthy bones and teeth. They are particularly important for growing children, pregnant and lactating women, as well as for people with active lifestyles.
