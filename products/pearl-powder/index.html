Description

Heritage Pure Medicinal Pearl Powder is processed stringently, and contains only 100% natural pearl powder—not a mixture of the inner oyster shells. Pearls are listed in the classics of Chinese medicine for their beauty and medicinal benefits that help revitalise the skin’s natural fairness and attractiveness.
