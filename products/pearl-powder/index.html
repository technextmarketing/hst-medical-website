What it is

Heritage Pure Medicinal Pearl Powder is processed stringently, and contains only 100% natural pearl powder—not a mixture of the inner oyster shells. It is sourced from medicinal pearls to ensure only the purest quality. Pearls are listed in the classics of Chinese medicine for their beauty and medicinal benefits that help revitalise the skin’s natural fairness and attractiveness.

How to use
  • Oral Intake: Adults: Take 1 to 2 vegicaps to be taken in the morning before food. Children under 12 yrs old: Half the adult dose. The contents of half a vegicap may be mixed with milk or warm water, once daily. Consult a physician for children under the age of 12 months before use. Avoid prolonged usage and overdose. Topical Use: Pearl powder contents in the vegicap may be mixed with moisturisers or Vitamin E cream and applied on face or hands
