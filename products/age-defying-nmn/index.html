What it is

Heritage Age-Defying NMN has a unique all-in-one formula just for your skin. As we age, NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) level decreases in our body. NMN (nicotinamide mononucleotide) helps to increase NAD+ level which supports stamina and energises our body, resulting in a youthful appearance and maintaining healthy eyes & cognitive abilities. Enriched with hydrolysed marine collagen, Heritage Age-Defying NMN decreases melanin and wrinkles while hydrating and promoting skin elasticity and firmness, leaving your skin smooth and supple. Fortified with L-glutathione, pearl powder and vitamin C, it brightens and revitalises the skin’s natural fairness to promote a radiant and luminous complexion from within.

How to use
  • Consume 1 to 2 sachets daily either by pouring into the mouth with or without water OR mixing into juices or other beverages.
