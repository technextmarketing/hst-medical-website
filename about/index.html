About HST Medical

Established in 1994, HST Medical is a manufacturer and supplier of health supplements and pain relief remedies to major pharmacies and online marketplaces.

Contemporary pharmaceuticals, traditional benefits

HST Medical products pair contemporary pharmaceuticals with the long-term benefits of natural nutrients. The pharmacists and TCM physicians behind the range develop formulas for modern needs from ethically sourced ingredients around the world.

Quality and safety first

From research and development to packaging and manufacturing, HST Medical follows authority guidelines, approved procedures and inspected premises. Ingredients are tested for authenticity, finished products are verified for safety, and third-party manufacturing is audited.

A heritage that began in 1930

The name is a tribute to Heng Say Tong, the medical hall established by the family of the company's co-founders. HST Medical Pte Ltd was incorporated in 1994 and draws on nearly a century of heritage in healthcare.

Part of Kowa Pharmaceutical Asia

On 29 May 2026 HST Medical became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Kowa Pharmaceutical Asia Pte. Ltd., part of Japan's Kowa group, whose consumer brands include Vantelin and Three Dimension Mask. Kowa's research depth joins HST Medical's range, while the HST Medical name and the brands on the shelf stay the same.
