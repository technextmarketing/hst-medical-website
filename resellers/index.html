Trade and resellers

HST Medical supplies pharmacies, clinics, TCM halls, e-commerce sellers and overseas distributors directly. Tell us about your business and a territory manager will be in touch within two working days.

What a trade account includes

  1. A territory manager. One contact for orders, re-orders and promotions.
  2. Product sheets. Benefits, usage, active ingredients, item codes and pack configurations for all 51 products.
  3. Trade pricing. Single, twin, triple, value and travel packs with tiered pricing.
  4. Shelf support. Point-of-sale material, product training and seasonal campaigns.

Already a reseller?

Email resellercontact@hstmedical.com or contact your territory manager. A self-service portal with order history and one-click re-orders is proposed for Phase 2.
